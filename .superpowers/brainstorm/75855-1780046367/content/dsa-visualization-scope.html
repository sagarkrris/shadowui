My recommendation

  • Start with A plus a small piece of C.
  • It gives immediate interview value without building a huge graph editor first.
  • Later, the playground can grow from the same visualization engine.

Scope guard

  • A full Visualgo clone is too large for one pass.
  • We should launch with 6-8 high-value patterns first.
  • Each pattern needs animation, explanation, quiz, and mock bridge.
