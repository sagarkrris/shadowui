A. Interview Pattern Theater

Step-by-step animated walkthroughs with pointer badges, map/stack/queue panels, narration, invariant, and “what to say in interview.” Best for confidence and memorability.

B. Algorithm Playground

More Visualgo-like: custom inputs, speed slider, play/pause, draggable nodes later. More interactive, but bigger scope and less interview-guided unless we add coaching.

2. Visual dry run

C. Guided DSA Mission

A mini-course flow for every pattern: lesson, visualization, selected-stack code, quiz, then mock. Strong learning path, but slower than A for quick interview reps.

My recommendation

Start with A plus a little of C: make every pattern visually alive, add selected tech-stack code templates, and keep a short “remember this in interview” checklist. That fixes the boring feeling without turning this into a huge separate app.
